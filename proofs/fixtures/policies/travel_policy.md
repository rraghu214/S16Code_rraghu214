# Travel reimbursement policy

Domestic hotel reimbursement is capped at ₹7,500 per night before tax. Economy rail or economy airfare is reimbursable. Any flight booked fewer than 7 calendar days before departure requires director approval. Meals are capped at ₹1,200 per travel day. Alcohol and minibar charges are never reimbursable. Claims must include itemised receipts.
